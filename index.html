The Industry Problem

Manual material handling
is costing your business.

Every day your workers push heavy loads manually, your operations lose time, money, and capacity. We fix that.

⚠️

Labour Shortages

Finding and retaining skilled workers for material movement is increasingly difficult and expensive across Indian industries.

😓

Worker Fatigue & Injuries

Manual load handling leads to musculoskeletal injuries, fatigue, absenteeism, and reduced productivity over time.

🐢

Slow Material Flow

Delays in moving materials between stations create bottlenecks that stall production lines and increase lead times.

💸

Rising Labour Costs

Manual transport requires multiple workers per shift. Labour costs compound annually while productivity stays flat.

⏱️

Unplanned Downtime

Material delays stop machines. Production halts while equipment waits for parts, components, or finished goods to arrive.

📉

Workflow Inefficiency

Without structured material movement, factory floors run on informal systems that are impossible to measure or improve.

About Hastin Machines

20 years of engineering behind every machine.

Hastin Machines is the industrial mobility brand of Shree Balaji Engineering Works — a precision engineering company with over two decades of manufacturing experience serving automotive, oil & gas, agricultural, and construction industries.

We launched Hastin Machines because we understood the problem from the factory floor up. Our engineers have spent years building precision components. Now we're applying that same engineering discipline to solve India's material movement challenge.

Every Hastin machine is built on the same foundation: heavy-gauge steel, copper-wound motors, and engineering processes refined over 20 years of manufacturing for global standards.
